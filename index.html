💡 What whyd Does

When a computer freezes or shuts down on its own, the reason is almost always written down somewhere in a log file. The problem is that these log files are long, technical and hard to read. Most people never find out what actually went wrong.

whyd solves that problem. It opens the log of your previous session, looks through it for the moments that matter, and shows you a short, clear summary on a single screen. Instead of scrolling through thousands of lines of text, you get a plain answer to a simple question: what happened to my computer?

It is designed to be the very first thing you run after a surprise crash or an unexpected shutdown, so you can stop guessing and start fixing.

✨ Key Features

  • One-screen summary. All the important findings are collected in a single view, so you do not need to hunt through pages of text.
  • Reads the previous boot. whyd focuses on the session that ended badly, not the one you are using right now.
  • Highlights memory problems. Out-of-memory situations are a very common cause of freezes, and whyd makes them easy to spot.
  • Clear error grouping. Repeated messages are grouped together so the noise disappears and the real cause stands out.
  • Built for the terminal. It runs right where you already work, with no desktop environment required.
  • Fast and lightweight. There is no background service to install and no long setup process.
  • Helpful for sharing. The summary is short enough to copy into a forum post when you ask for help.

🖥️ How to Use It

You do not need to be a programmer to use whyd. The idea is simple: after your computer has restarted from a crash, open your terminal and start whyd. It will do the reading for you.

Once the summary appears, look for the lines that are marked as problems. These are the messages that explain what went wrong. If you see a note about memory, your computer likely ran out of available memory. If you see a note about a device or a service, that component is the likely culprit.

You can move through the results, close the summary and reopen it at any time. Nothing is changed on your system: whyd only reads information that already exists.

If you want a second opinion, copy the short summary and share it with a friend or on a support forum. Because the output is condensed, it is far easier for someone else to understand than a full log file.

🎯 Who It Is For

whyd is made for anyone who uses a Linux desktop or laptop and has ever been left staring at a black screen wondering what happened. That includes students, office workers, hobbyists and system administrators who want a quick answer instead of a long investigation.

It is especially useful if you run a system that keeps its logs in the standard systemd journal, which is the default on most modern Linux distributions, including Arch Linux.

❓ Common Questions

Does whyd fix my computer?

No. whyd explains what happened. It does not repair anything, because the right repair depends on the cause it finds. Think of it as a clear diagnosis rather than a repair tool.

Will it change my files or settings?

No. whyd only reads existing log information. It does not modify, delete or upload anything.

Do I need to be online to use it?

Only for the download. After that, whyd works entirely on your own computer.

What if the summary is empty?

An empty summary usually means the previous session ended cleanly, or that it was not saved. Try again right after a shutdown that happened unexpectedly.

Is it hard to install?
